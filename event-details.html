About the Event

Campus Innovation Day is our university's preeminent forum for cross-pollinating collegiate breakthroughs with applied industry solutions. Over twenty student-led laboratory cohorts will unveil working prototypes spanning clean-energy transport, assistive health algorithms, embedded environmental sensors, and augmented reality learning tools.

Attendees will have hands-on access to live hardware exhibits, open-source code repositories, and physical bench testing stations situated throughout the University Main Hall. Whether evaluating thesis prototypes or participating in real-time user-acceptance assessments, visitors are invited into an authentic active research environment.

The mid-day keynote from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering will unpack institutional methodologies for ethical machine learning deployment. The event concludes with an audience-voted fast-pitch tournament awarding micro-grants for undergraduate venture validation.

  • 32+ Live Prototypes
  • $12k Venture Grants
  • 14 Research Labs

Agenda & Timetable

Schedule subject to minor room adjustments

  1. 10:00 AM Main Foyer

    Check-in & Morning Welcome

    Badge collection, distribution of commemorative programs, morning coffee, and introductory directory browsing.

  2. 10:30 AM Auditorium A

    Faculty Keynote: The Future of AI in Campus Research

    Presented by Dr. Alistair Vance, Chair of Machine Intelligence. Exploring institutional guardrails, sovereign lab compute, and student-driven validation.

  3. 11:45 AM Exhibition Hall

    Student Project Demonstrations & Poster Session

    Interactive booth walkthroughs across biomedical technology, automated robotics, urban agriculture devices, and distributed computing models.

  4. 1:00 PM Courtyard Pavilion

    Networking Lunch & Mentor Breakouts

    Complimentary catered artisanal buffet. Round-table discussions facilitated by industry mentors, intellectual property advisors, and venture alumni.

  5. 2:00 PM Auditorium A

    Innovation Pitch Competition & Audience Voting

    Six shortlisted student startup teams deliver 4-minute rapid presentations followed by real-time audience voting via credentialed terminal links.

  6. 2:45 PM Auditorium A

    Awards Ceremony & Closing Remarks

    Presentation of seed capital awards, Dean’s Excellence Medals, and official publication of the 2026 Academic Innovation Digest.
